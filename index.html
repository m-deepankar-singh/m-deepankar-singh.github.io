 CASE STUDY № 2
INFER: a serverless platform where sleeping models wake in one second

A multi-tenant GPU platform built on a tiered-warmth ladder — models sleep in pinned host RAM at ~3% of GPU cost, wake in ~1s, and survive Spot interruptions with a tested failover state machine.

solo build · 40ms warm TTFT · ~1.0s wake · 210s → 12.7s
